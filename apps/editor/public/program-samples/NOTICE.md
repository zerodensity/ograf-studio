# Program monitor sample loops

The news and entertainment studio backgrounds in this folder are original procedural animations
created for OGraf Studio. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

They contain no footage, audio, logos, or third-party imagery. They are bundled for offline preview
and are never added to an exported graphic.
Regenerate them with `scripts/generateProgramSamples.ps1` and FFmpeg with libx264.
